# Book sections stand in numeric order; an edition's cross-chapter rearrangement is furniture

Charles 1912 prints the Epistle of Enoch out of sequence (92 → 91:1–11, 18–19 → 93 → 91:12–17 → 94), and the source research said "keep his printed order; don't renumber". `reading` (ADR 0013) walks one section only, and section order freezes with the grid at first release.

**Decision:** sections stand in chapter-number order, each chapter's verses in numeric order; no Book-level walk. The numbering is Dillmann's, shared by every edition (Knibb, Isaac, Nickelsburg/VanderKam), and all of them print numeric order — the rearrangement is Charles's reconstruction, of which 4Q212 later confirmed only the 93 → 91:12–17 half. His section Headings stay verbatim on the atoms they precede, so the dislocation is visible without being walked. "Don't renumber" is honoured; "keep his printed order" is not, at the cross-chapter level only (within-chapter interleaving, ADR 0010, stands).

**Rejected:** page order at section level (92, 91, 93 — loses the split of 91); a Book-level `reading` list (Title Bar steps 93 → 91 → 94 and a second reading concept for one editor's conjecture); renumbering.
