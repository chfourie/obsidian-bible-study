Research for building a book module (precedent: [humility-source-text.md](humility-source-text.md)). Date: 2026-09-08. All claims cite the primary source consulted; unverified points are flagged inline.

## Summary & Recommendation

- **Apparatus (1912 Clarendon, Charles’s own key)**: transcribed below from the Internet Archive page image of Cornell’s 1912 Clarendon copy ([cu31924067146773](https://archive.org/details/cu31924067146773), IIIF leaf `$115` = the unnumbered abbreviations page between half-title and p. 1). Brackets/symbols Charles defines there: **⌈ ⌉**, **⌈⌈ ⌉⌉**, **〈 〉**, **[ ]**, **( )**, **thick type**. The 1912 key page does **not** gloss **† †** or **…**; those two lines appear in the SPCK 1917 reprint key, though †† is used in the 1912 translation body (e.g. CVIII.4).

## Charles’s apparatus key (1912 Clarendon — from page image)

Source: IA Cornell 1912, abbreviations page (IIIF `https://iiif.archive.org/iiif/cu31924067146773$115/full/full/0/default.jpg`; half-title leaf `$114` reads only “THE BOOK OF ENOCH”). Cross-checked against Toronto Robarts OCR of the same page ([bookofenochor1en00char](https://archive.org/details/bookofenochor1en00char)). Spelling/diacritics normalized to the printed page; OCR alone is unreliable for the bracket glyphs.

### Witnesses & MS sigla

| Siglum | Meaning Charles assigns |
|---|---|
| **a, b, c, d, &c.** | the Ethiopic MSS. |
| **α** | the earlier Ethiopic MSS., i.e. **g g¹ m q t u**. |
| **β** | the later Ethiopic MSS., i.e. **a b c d, &c.** (i.e. from *a* to *z a b*, with the exception of the earlier MSS.). |
| **α–g** | all the MSS. of the α class but *g*, and so on. |
| **β–a b c** | all the MSS. of the β class but *a*, *b*, and *c*, and so on. |
| **E** | the Ethiopic Version. |
| **Gˢ** | the fragments of the Greek Version preserved in Syncellus; in the case of 8ᵇ–9ᵇ there are two forms of the text, **Gˢ¹ Gˢ²**. |
| **Gᵍ** | the large fragment of the Greek Version discovered at Akhmîm, and deposited in the Gizeh Museum, Cairo. |

(Common ASCII aliases in digitizations: `Gs`/`G^s` = Gˢ; `Gg`/`G^g` = Gᵍ.)

### Brackets & symbols (translation)

Charles’s heading: *The following brackets are used in the translation of 1 Enoch:*

| Mark | Meaning Charles assigns |
|---|---|
| **⌈ ⌉** | words so enclosed are found in **Gᵍ** but not in **E**. |
| **⌈⌈ ⌉⌉** | words so enclosed are found in **E** but not in **Gᵍ** or **Gˢ**. |
| **〈 〉** | words so enclosed are **restored**. |
| **[ ]** | words so enclosed are **interpolations**. |
| **( )** | words so enclosed are **supplied by the editor**. |
| **thick type** | words so printed are **emended**. |

The 1912 abbreviations page **ends** with the thick-type sentence. It does **not** print definitions for **† †** or **…**.

### † † and … (used in 1912 text; glossed in SPCK 1917 key only)

- In the 1912 translation body Charles marks corruptions with daggers (e.g. CVIII.4 OCR/page tradition: “I could not †look over†” — Cornell OCR `tlook overt`; PG `†look over†`).
- No definition of †† or lacuna-points was found on the 1912 abbreviations page or elsewhere in a quick scan of the 1912 front matter ([cu31924067146773](https://archive.org/details/cu31924067146773) OCR).
- The SPCK 1917 popular reprint (which states it reprints the 1912 translation) adds two glosses to its shortened key ([sacred-texts boe003](https://archive.sacred-texts.com/bib/boe/boe003.htm); [pg77935.txt](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/77935/pg77935.txt)):
  - **† †** = corruption in the text.
  - **…** (a series of points) = some words which have been lost.
- SPCK’s key also **drops** the Ethiopic MS class sigla (α/β and individual letters) — expected, since the popular reprint omits the full critical notes.

**Build rule**: treat †† / … meanings as Charles’s practice as glossed for the reprint; when verifying marks, prefer 1912 page images over any digitization’s bracket choice.

## What the 1912 volume is

R. H. Charles, *The Book of Enoch, or 1 Enoch*, Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1912 — “not so much a second edition as a new book” vs his 1893 Dillmann-based edition (Preface, 1 June 1912, in [cu31924067146773](https://archive.org/details/cu31924067146773)). Translation made from Charles’s 1906 Ethiopic text; volume interleaves section introductions, translation, and critical/exegetical notes, plus Greek fragments in an appendix. SPCK 1917 (*Translations of Early Documents*) reprints **only the translation** (with Oesterley’s introduction), by permission of Charles and the Clarendon Press Delegates ([pg77935.txt](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/77935/pg77935.txt) Editors’ thanks note).

## Structure → Module Atoms (PG plain text)

- **Top-level units**: Editors’ Preface; Oesterley Introduction; Abbreviations key; translation sections (I–XXXVI Watchers/Journeys; XXXVII–LXXI Parables + Noah fragment; LXXII–LXXXII Luminaries; LXXXIII–XC Dream-Visions; XCI–CV Admonitions; CVI–CVII Noah fragment; CVIII Appendix).
- **Chapters**: roman numerals `I.`–`CVIII.` with verse numbers `1.` …; some chapters open with italicized `_Title_` lines.
- **Apparatus atoms** (preserve in module markup):
  - Version brackets → span/classes (`gg-not-e`, `e-not-g`, `restored`, `interpolation`, `editorial`).
  - `=emended=` → emended/thick.
  - `†…†` → corruption.
  - `...` → lacuna.
- **Note**: Charles’s chapter order sometimes prints XCI/XCII/XCIII out of numeric sequence (Apocalypse of Weeks dislocation) — keep his printed order; don’t renumber.

## Licensing

- **Charles translation (1912)**: published UK/US 1912 (pre-1930 US) → **public domain in the US**. Author R. H. Charles d. 1931 → PD in life+70 jurisdictions since end of 2001. PG marks #77935 “Public domain in the USA” ([gutenberg.org/ebooks/77935](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/77935)).
- **Oesterley introduction (1917)**: US PD as pre-1930 publication; Oesterley d. 1950 → life+70 PD from 2021. Optional to ship with the module; not required for the Enoch text.
- **PG terms**: strip PG license and all “Project Gutenberg” references → “a text unrestricted by U.S. intellectual property law” ([gutenberg.org/policy/license.html](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html)). Keeping the PG name would forbid the normalizations we need (bracket codepoints, typography).
- **May we ship as a GitHub release artifact?** **Yes** — Charles’s English (and the SPCK reprint of it) is clear of US copyright; strip PG boilerplate; optional courtesy provenance credit in module metadata (same rule as Humility / BSB precedent).

## Recommendation

**Build from PG #77935 plain text** ([pg77935.txt](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/77935/pg77935.txt)). Reasons: only complete hand-proofed single-file digitization of Charles’s English through CVIII with apparatus marks largely intact; licensing unambiguous once PG wrapper is stripped; matches the Humility pattern (PG build + IA page-image verify).

**Verify** disputed readings and glyph choices against the **1912 Clarendon page images** ([cu31924067146773](https://archive.org/details/cu31924067146773) / [bookofenochor1en00char](https://archive.org/details/bookofenochor1en00char)) — especially bracket *type* (⌈⌉ vs [] vs 〈〉) and ††. Use Charles’s **1912** abbreviations page (transcribed above) as the normative key for E / Gˢ / Gᵍ / α / β; use the SPCK two-line gloss for †† and … when documenting those marks.

Do **not** build from Wikisource (incomplete front matter) or sacred-texts alone (known apparatus slips) or raw 1912 OCR.
